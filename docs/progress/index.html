What this tells us

The existing learning rule can improve the gather-and-eat sequence with enough practice in this fixture.

What it doesn’t tell us

The food was nearby. These results do not establish navigation around walls, sustained survival, useful language or intelligence outside the game.

“Starting” already had 6,144 decisions of training per brain. It is not an untrained or random control.
