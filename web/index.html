Learned communication and reproduction are still under development. Amber fruit hurts when eaten; thorns hurt when walked on or handled. Health recovers slowly after injury and creatures cannot die in this development world. Hunger below 60 reduces rest recovery; below 20 weakens movement. Prolonged zero fullness causes brief fainting. Eating relieves hunger. Helping a hungry neighbor up shares one food if you have it.
