## October 6, 2026 — Preserve complete lives through failures

- Reject incomplete live checkpoints without recreating missing controllers.
- Stop maximum-speed batches immediately when a controller fails.
- Restore all decision state after failure, including optimizer updates, and
  retain queued player actions for an explicit retry.
- Protect the last complete checkpoint if rollback or a world transition fails.
- Pass 231 tests and verify an unchanged complete checkpoint after installation.
  Seven findings remain in the [audit](docs/AUDIT-2026-10-06.md).
